Why this page exists

Right now you rent your order button

Krazy Moose Subs has no website of its own. A customer searching for you lands on Facebook, Yelp, TripAdvisor, order.online, akdelivered.com, DoorDash or Uber Eats — every one of them a page you do not control, and most of them taking a cut of the order. One consequence is visible in public: the same sandwich is listed at a different price on every platform.

Prices as publicly listed by each platform for a 10″ Cheese Steak at the time this demo was built. These are the platforms' listed figures, not the shop's counter price — which is exactly the problem.
