Preview accessibility information

Accessibility

This design preview includes text labels for form fields, keyboard focus indicators, a skip link, and reduced-motion styling. Decorative illustrations do not convey essential information. The inquiry form is deliberately disabled; it is not a route to contact the business.

Motion and media

The preview responds to your system's reduced-motion preference. No first-party video or audio was present at this review. Future video with speech or meaningful sound needs accurate captions and equivalent text or audio description where needed; publishing video alone is not an accessibility pass.

Known limits and reporting barriers

This is a basic accessibility improvement, not a WCAG certification or guarantee. Third-party maps and linked pages have separate interfaces. Full assistive-technology, cognitive accessibility, content accuracy and manual contrast review remain incomplete.

Report barriers to Digital Consulting Studios at digitalconsultingstudios@gmail.com, including the page and issue. This publisher contact is not Krazy Moose Subs's business inbox. No response-time promise is made.
